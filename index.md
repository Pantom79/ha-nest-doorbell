# HA Nest Doorbell

HA Nest Doorbell is a small personal app that connects the owner's own Google Nest Doorbell to the owner's own Home Assistant installation through the Google Smart Device Management API. It is used by a single person for a private smart home and is not offered to other users.

Die App "HA Nest Doorbell" verbindet die Nest Doorbell des Betreibers mit seiner eigenen Home-Assistant-Installation (Google Smart Device Management API). Sie wird nur privat von einer Person genutzt und nicht an Dritte angeboten.
